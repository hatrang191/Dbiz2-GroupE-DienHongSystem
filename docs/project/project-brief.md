## Problem

Nurses are overloaded: there are many residents but only a limited number of nurses, so it is
difficult to monitor every resident effectively. Care given and condition changes are written
down inconsistently, so the next shift lacks reliable information. Families only hear about their
relative's condition when they call or visit, alerts reach them late, and there is no simple way
to send a question to the staff and get an answer. Resident and family information is recorded by hand at reception and is hard to keep up to date, and the Board of Directors has no summary of residents' condition and care activity, so management decisions rely on verbal reports.

## Goal of the project

Nurses see the condition of their residents in one place and receive an alert at once when a
resident needs attention, families follow their relative's condition and are told promptly about
an alert, and the Board of Directors can review reports on care and operations. Measures agreed with the client are in [the CVCA](../context/cvca.md) (stakeholder requirements) and, in money terms, in [W6 cash flow](../function-list/w6-cash-flow.md).
